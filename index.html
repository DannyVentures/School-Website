About Us

Brief History:

Kingdom Life College of Education, located on Ikot Udoma Estate Road, Eket, was founded in 2019 by Dr. Godwin Osung with the support of Kingdom Life University, USA

The College was established with the sole aim of traning professionals in the field of education at the Nigerian Certificate in Education (NCE) and Degree levels.

Since its inception, the college has trained and graduated hundreds of teachers who are making meaningful contributions to education across Nigeria.
